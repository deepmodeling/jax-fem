------------
Description
------------

The development of large residual elastic strains and stresses during laser
powder-bed fusion (LPBF) additive manufacturing is one of the most significant
barriers to widespread adoption. Accurate modeling of these strains and stresses
is broadly recognized as an effective tool for mitigating these challenges, but
rigorous validation data are needed. This data publication includes measurement
data from neutron diffraction and synchrotron X-ray diffraction characterizations 
of residual elastic strains in as-built (not heat treated) artifacts manufactured 
as part of the 2018 Additive Manufacturing Benchmark Series (AM Bench). 
Comprehensive details about AM Bench and the measurement data released in 2018 
and 2022 may be found at
<https://www.nist.gov/ambench>.

This collection is documented in:
T. Q. Phan, M. Strantza, M. R. Hill, T. H. Gnaupel-Herold, J. Heigel,  
C. R. D'Elia, A. T. DeWald, B. Clausen, D. C. Pagan, J. Y. Peter Ko, D. W. Brown, 
& L. E. Levine (2019). Elastic Residual Strain and Stress Measurements and 
Corresponding Part Deflections of 3D Additive Manufacturing Builds of IN625 
AM Bench Artifacts Using Neutron Diffraction, Synchrotron X-Ray Diffraction, and 
Contour Method. Integrating Materials and Manufacturing Innovation, 8(3), 
318-334. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s40192-019-00149-0>

Data Overview
-------------

For convenience, some of the more common filename prefix definitions are listed below, 
in approximate order encountered.
  
  IN625 - a nickel-based super alloy
  15-5 - a precipitation hardenable, martensitic stainless steel
  45 - designates a sample direction halfway between the X and Z directions
  EDXRD - energy dispersive X-ray diffraction

Data Formats
-------------

.fig - A proprietary binary figure file for MATLAB. A .fig file is basically a 
hierarchical structure of all the non-default properties of the stored figure and 
its descendant handles, stored within a simple MATLAB MAT file. Details about the 
MAT file format may be found at 
<https://www.mathworks.com/help/pdf_doc/matlab/matfile_format.pdf>.

.pdf - Portable Document Format (PDF) is a standard representation
of documents independent of software, hardware, and OS. Specifications for 
the PDF format may be found at https://www.pdfa.org/resource/pdf-specification-index/

.png - PNG (Portable Network Graphics), is an extensible file format for the 
lossless, portable, well-compressed storage of raster images. Detailed information 
may be found at <https://www.w3.org/TR/PNG/>.

.txt - Plain text that supports standard ASCII characters, including numbers, 
symbols, and spaces, but does not support any type of text formatting.
